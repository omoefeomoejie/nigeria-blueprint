# What We Believe

*Nine lines. Short enough to carry in your mouth, and each one backed by
something you can go and check.*

---

## Why this book is different

You have heard plans before. Power will be fixed. Roads will come. The
economy will be diversified. Every four years, the same sentences.

What you have never been given is the working. The evidence. The names of
the states where it was tried. What it cost. What would prove it wrong.

So this book makes one promise that no manifesto makes, and it is the ninth
line: don't believe us, check it. The claims are published. So are the
sources, the confidence levels, and something no political document
includes, a list of everywhere we were wrong.

We were wrong about Lagos. We thought it proved a tax reform other states
could copy, until we compared it with Taraba and saw that what Lagos really
has is a seaport and a third of the national economy. We were wrong about
what drove Enugu's money, we said automation, the decomposition said asset
recovery. We were wrong about Vietnam. All three reversals are written down,
dated, public.

That is the point. A movement that cannot change its mind where people can
see becomes an ideology, and ideology has never governed anything well.

## 1. The Feeding Rule

> *A government that eats from your work must answer your voice. A government
> that eats oil answers nobody.*

Thirty of thirty-six states get between 71 and 92 per cent of their money
from oil shared in Abuja [Certified, Node 1]. Think about what that means. A
government funded whether or not you work, whether or not you are safe,
whether or not your children learn.

It is not that our leaders are uniquely wicked. It is that nothing in the
arrangement makes serving you the way to keep power. Change what the state
eats and you change what it notices. → *Chapter 1*

## 2. The Three Jobs

> *Block the leak. Plan the money. Show the work.*

Lagos gets its money from ports and companies. Enugu got its from recovering
assets nobody was using. Opposite sources, and neither one can be copied by
a state without ports or assets.

But underneath both, the same three jobs [Certified, Node 1]. Stop the money
leaking on its way in. Budget it towards building rather than salaries. Then
show, in public, what got built. The source is luck. The three jobs are the
part any state can copy. → *Chapters 1, 4*

## 3. The Builder's Rule

> *Don't build on one good man. Build what a bad man cannot scatter.*

One state, one handover, two reforms. The teacher competency testing stopped
the month the governor left. The open government machinery not only survived,
it expanded [Certified, Nodes 2 and 4].

Same state. Same successor. The difference was not which reform was better.
It was that one lived in law with people outside government defending it, and
the other lived in one man's will. → *the irreversibility test in every
chapter*

## 4. The Seat Rule

> *Test the person before the seat, and let the law hold the test, not the oga.*

Kaduna tested twenty-one thousand teachers against a primary four standard
and sacked those who failed. It survived a strike, a court case, and sacking
the teachers' union president himself [Certified, Node 2].

Then the governor left and the testing stopped. The test had no home in law
and no constituency to defend it. Publish the test before anyone sits. Put a
statutory board in charge. And we should say plainly, no Nigerian case has
yet shown that testing teachers made children learn more. That is still owed
[Node 2]. → *Chapter 2*

## 5. The Trace Rule

> *Money you can trace is money that can reach you.*

Kaduna holds a perfect independent transparency score, 39 out of 39
[Certified, Node 4]. Around the world, publishing contracts increases
competition and cuts corruption risk.

But publishing is only the first of four steps. The others are making the
information usable, making participation actually binding, and measuring
what was saved. Nigeria has built the first and none of the rest. That is a
job of work, not a slogan. → *Chapter 4*

## 6. The Ground and the Badge

> *Safety comes when those who know the ground join those who hold the power,
> under law.*

In Borno, local young men drove Boko Haram out of Maiduguri when federal
soldiers alone could not hold it, because they could tell a neighbour from an
infiltrator [Certified, Node 3]. The same researchers who record that also
record the abuses those men committed.

So it is all three or none. Local knowledge, lawful force, complaints
published where anyone can read them. → *Chapter 3*

## 7. The Doors Rule

> *Every wall has a door somewhere. Take the open doors first.*

The formula for sharing Nigeria's money has not changed since 1992, because
every proposal to change it must pass through the desk of the tier that
loses. In 2022 a full proposal reached the President and was simply never
sent forward [Certified, Node 5].

Yet in 2023 the Constitution was amended so states could generate their own
electricity, and it passed every gate. Why? Because that door cost the centre
a headache, not money. Learn which gate is held by whom, take what is open
now, and organise for the rest. → *Chapter 5*

## 8. The Medicine Rule

> *Blame builds nothing. We did not come to fight anybody, we came to build.*

Tribe and religion are not Nigeria's disease. How people describe themselves
here swings twenty points in eighteen months, rises at election time, and
hardens when government treats them unfairly [Certified, Node 6].

People shelter in tribe when the state fails them. Politicians reach for it
when they are fighting over one central pot of money. Fix the state, shrink
the pot, and the shelter is needed less. Fight your neighbour and nothing
gets fixed at all. → *Chapters 5, 6*

## 9. The Signature

> *Don't believe us, check it. Everything is written, so anybody can test it.*

The strongest evidence in this book is not ours and not foreign. Nigeria's
own youth service scheme, fifty years old, measurably reduces ethnic
prejudice in the people it posts far from home, and the effect lasts years
[Certified, Node 6].

We did not invent that. We went looking, found it, checked it, and wrote down
what would prove us wrong. → *the Research Engine, and every kill condition*

## The Wager

> *When enough of us know the same truth and move, the country moves.*

**Untested.** This one is our bet, not our finding [Part V]. Everything else
in this book has to name its evidence. This belief cannot yet name any, so we
label it and leave it standing.

---

## What this asks of you

Carry one line. Check one number about your own state. Join one thing that
puts you among people unlike you, since that is the only instruction here
with proof behind it.

And if you find us wrong, say so in public. We keep a file of our own errors.
Yours will go in it with your name on it.
