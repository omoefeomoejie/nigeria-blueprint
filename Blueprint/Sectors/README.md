# Sectors — The Vertical Axis

## What this is

Chapters 1–7 are the **horizontal axis**: the state's operating capabilities
— revenue, selection, accountability, security, constitutional routes,
identity, learning. They cut across every ministry.

This folder is the **vertical axis**: ministry by ministry, sector by
sector. Agriculture, power, health, transport, trade.

The two meet in a matrix. Verticals are applications; horizontals are the
operating system beneath them. That is why the horizontals were written
first: most sector plans in Nigeria fail not for sector reasons but for the
same four unstated ones — inputs leak, budgets don't convert to delivery,
officials are selected by patronage, and nobody measures outcomes.

## Why this is research, not encyclopedia

If twenty sectors each decompose into the same root causes, Part II's first
principle is confirmed by convergence from twenty independent starting
points — far stronger than seven nodes we chose ourselves.

If a sector *doesn't* fit — if it fails for reasons the horizontals cannot
explain — that is a genuine finding and Layer 4 gets updated.

Either result is evidence. That is what keeps this falsifiable rather than
merely long.

## The five-field template

Sector briefs are deliberately lighter than horizontal chapters. They
inherit the machinery rather than repeat it.

1. **What the sector produces now** — real numbers, sourced.
2. **What is broken** — decomposed into parts, not one lump.
3. **Which horizontal failure explains each part** — cite the chapter.
4. **What is genuinely sector-specific** — the residue the horizontals do
   not explain. This is the part worth original work.
5. **What one state could try in 18 months** — with a kill condition.

## The rule

**No sector brief ships without at least one certified Nigerian number and
one named case.** If a brief could be written from general knowledge alone,
it does not get written. This is the only thing standing between a vertical
axis and a bigger warehouse.

## Status: six briefs written, run closed

See **`00_Synthesis.md`** for what the six collectively proved: one addition
to the diagnosis (absorptive capacity), one refinement (it is a distribution,
not a condition — 17 clean states vs 5 holding 97.5% of abandonments), and
two corrections to our own chapters (5 and 7).

The run was stopped at six deliberately. The horizontal explanations had
begun repeating and the marginal finding was falling; completing eight for
symmetry would have been warehouse-building.

## Two corrections to the original sector list

**Elections is not a sector.** It is the selection mechanism for everyone in
every other chapter, and belongs on the horizontal axis beside leadership
selection. Logged as the leading candidate for **Node 8**.

**Diplomacy is federal-only.** A state's sole handle is investment
attraction, which lives inside the trade brief. No standalone chapter.
