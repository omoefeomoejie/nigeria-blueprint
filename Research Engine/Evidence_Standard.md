# Evidence Standard — v0.2

How a claim earns each status. Nothing in the Blueprint may be stated more
confidently than its evidence allows.

**v0.2 change (governance):** added **Rejected**. A disproven hypothesis is a
finding, not an open question, and must not decay into "Contested" or vanish.

## The five statuses

| Status | Bar |
|---|---|
| **Certified** | Confirmed by an independent, disinterested source (bureau data, civic audit, peer-reviewed work). |
| **Contested** | Real underlying data, but the *interpretation* comes from interested parties, or credible sources conflict. |
| **Confounded** | Outcome is real but cannot be attributed to the proposed cause; an uncopyable factor could explain it. |
| **Owed** | A specific named fact or source would settle the question, and we have not yet retrieved it. |
| **Rejected** | Investigated and found false. Logged permanently; never deleted. A rejected hypothesis is gold — it stops the same wrong path being walked twice. |

*(Candidate status "Strong" — multiple credible sources awaiting final
verification — is NOT adopted. No current claim needs a tier between Contested
and Certified that Owed cannot already express. It waits as a Candidate until a
real claim requires it.)*

## Fact vs. Interpretation — keep them separate (v0.2 patch)

Every status assignment must distinguish the two. A status is an *interpretation*;
it must be traceable to *facts* that are stated separately and plainly.

**Worked example (Node 1, Lagos):**
- **Facts:** Lagos IGR N815.86bn (2023, NBS); 28.1% FAAC-funded; ~1/3 of national
  GDP; ~70% of foreign-trade inflow; 800,000+ properties enumerated (LIRS).
- **Interpretation:** *Confounded* — the reform cannot be isolated from the
  endowment, because the GDP/trade concentration could alone explain the IGR.
- **Trace:** the interpretation rests on the GDP-concentration and Taraba-contrast
  facts, not on the reform facts. Stated this way, a reader can challenge the
  interpretation without disputing the facts.

## Rules
1. Every Blueprint claim carries its status.
2. Interested sources cannot certify; they establish that data exists, not why it moved.
3. Every Contested/Owed claim names the single fact that would close it.
4. Failure is evidence: a Rejected claim updates the engine exactly as a confirmation would.
5. A status is an interpretation and must trace to separately-stated facts.
