# Law Zero — The Engine Only Changes When Reality Teaches It

**The Law**

No skill, law, SOP, or rule may be added or edited except as the direct output
of a completed investigation, with a named case attached as its birth
certificate. If a proposed rule cannot point to the specific node where the old
process succeeded or failed, it does not enter the engine.

Not "sounds right." Not "feels rigorous." Traceable to a case, or out.

**Why this is Law Zero and not Law 13**

The earlier phase of this project produced a large, elegant architecture —
skills, laws, frameworks, a constitution of skills — and not one falsifiable
claim. We named that failure mode "the well-governed empty warehouse": perfect
shelving, no stock. Law Zero is the single rule that prevents the warehouse from
ever recurring, because it makes evidence the only currency that can buy a
change to the engine.

**The standing risk this guards against**

Building a world-class research engine is *more* seductive than doing the
research, because a reusable methodology flatters the intellect more than one
state's audit data. That seduction is the warehouse trap wearing a smarter
disguise. Law Zero is the tripwire.

**A corollary on generalisation**

The engine is built *for Nigeria*, ruthlessly specific. The hope that it might
later transform Kenya, Ghana, or Pakistan is a hypothesis, not a feature to
design for now. The methodology earns the word "reusable" only after it has
produced one complete, verified Blueprint. Generalisation is a reward for
finishing, never a goal that reshapes the work mid-flight.

---

**Origin:** Node 1 (Revenue Structure). The investigation produced more durable
value (how the project should work) than the data point it was chasing (Enugu).
That asymmetry is what revealed the engine needed a law protecting evidence-first
discipline before any skill was written.
