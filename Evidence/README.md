# Evidence

*Everything behind the book. A reader never needs to open this. A sceptic
can open all of it.*

## Start here

- **[FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md)** — everything established, ranked by what it
  changed, plus what we still do not know
- **[RED_TEAM.md](RED_TEAM.md)** — where we attack our own work and rank our
  weaknesses by how much damage they do
- **[MISTAKES.md](MISTAKES.md)** — sixteen recorded reversals, permanent,
  including three that correct our own earlier conclusions

## The investigations

`Nodes/` — nine investigations, each ending in a decision with a stated
confidence level and a list of what is still owed.

Revenue · Leadership · Security · Accountability · Constitution · Identity ·
Education · Elections · External constraint

## The sectors

`Sectors/` — twelve briefs, ministry by ministry, each testing the diagnosis
from outside. Agriculture, power, health, education, water, transport,
housing and land, oil and gas, the cost of governance, and the map of which
sector must move before which.

## The method

`Method/` — how we decide what is true. Law Zero, the Evidence Standard, the
ten rules, the Publication Rule, the real-terms restatement, our untested
assumptions.

## Chapters

`Chapters/` — the technical versions of the book's chapters, carrying the
evidence statuses and the "what we got wrong" notes that the book
compresses. These are the working documents the book was written from.
