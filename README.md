# Nigeria Blueprint

Evidence-first research engine and blueprint for Nigeria's national transformation.
Every rule traces to a named case; every claim carries its evidence status.

## Structure
- `Research Engine/` — the methodology machine. `MANIFEST.md` is the single source of truth for its contents. Governed by Law Zero: no rule enters without a completed investigation as its birth certificate.
- `Blueprint/` — the public-facing product (doctrine, diagnosis, chapters). Populated only from decision-grade nodes.

## Rules of this repo
1. Canonical author: Claude (Research Engine sessions). Reviews critique; one source writes.
2. Engine releases ship complete and are tagged (`v0.3`, `v0.4`, ...).
3. Cadence: engine commits and investigation (reality) commits alternate.
