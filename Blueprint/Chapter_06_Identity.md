# Chapter 6 — One People: Building Nigerians Out of Nigerians

*We do not ask anyone to stop being Igbo, Hausa, Yoruba, Ijaw, Tiv, Kanuri —
or Christian, or Muslim. We ask what makes being Nigerian worth more.*

---

## 1. Desired Outcome
By year 10, measured national-over-ethnic identification rises against a
published Afrobarometer baseline, reported ethnic discrimination falls, and
cross-regional mobility rises — with religion measured separately for
capture, not for belief.

## 2. Structural Matches
- **NYSC (Nigeria's own)** [Certified — causal]: quasi-random cross-ethnic
  posting raised willingness to relocate across ethnic lines 15–20pp and
  cut ethnic-favoritism preference, persisting ~5 years (Okunogbe 2024,
  AEJ:Applied). The strongest causal evidence in this Blueprint.
- **Tanzania** [Certified — mechanism, with caveats]: neutral language,
  school intermingling, early fair distribution, elite restraint.

## 3. The Mechanism
Identity here is **not ancient and not fixed**. Ethnic self-identification
in Nigeria moved ~20 points within eighteen months, waxes at elections, and
strengthens when the state discriminates [Certified]. Tribe is a *shelter*
from state failure and a *weapon* in central-pot competition. So the
mechanisms are: **contact** (proven), **a group-neutral language** (Nigeria
already has one — Pidgin), **fair distribution** (Nodes 1/5), and **elite
restraint** (make ethnic mobilization pay less).

## 4. Preconditions (Rule 5) — why Tanzania doesn't transfer wholesale
Swahili worked because it belonged to no dominant group; Ethiopia's Amharic
and Mobutu's Lingala failed on exactly that [Certified]. Tanzania also had
no dominant ethnic group and abolished chiefdoms — a route Nigeria cannot
and should not take. **The mechanisms transfer; the package does not.**

## 5. Nigerian Constraints
Three large groups; constitutionally entrenched federal character; powerful
traditional institutions; and an oil pot that makes ethnic competition
lucrative. Religion is the honest exception — a meaning system that
persisted even in Tanzania's success [Certified].

## 6. Nigerian Adaptation
**Strengthen the machine that already works.** Protect NYSC's cross-ethnic
posting integrity (the mechanism *is* the posting — waivers and
same-region placements destroy the effect), and extend contact below the
graduate tier: technical/vocational service, inter-regional secondary
exchanges, national sports and skills competitions with mandated mixing.
Adopt Pidgin deliberately in public communication as the group-neutral
language it already is. Route fair-distribution through Chapters 1 and 5.

## 7. Political Economy
Losers: politicians whose only asset is ethnic mobilization, and gatekeepers
of quota patronage. They will call this an attack on their people's share.
Counter-design: never propose removing group protections without
demonstrating the replacement is fairer *in delivery*; attack the
activation payoff (the pot), not the identity.

## 8. Early-Win Pilot
One state + NYSC partnership, 18 months: a documented cross-posting
integrity audit (how many corps members actually served outside their
region?) published — the first time that number is public — plus one
extended-contact cohort below graduate level with pre/post attitude
measurement.

## 9. Scale Strategy
Publish the cross-posting integrity figures nationally; states compete on
hosting quality. Contact scales by protocol, not by preaching.

## 10. Success Metrics
Year 1: posting-integrity baseline published. Year 3: pre/post attitude
shift in the extended-contact cohort matching NYSC's ~15pp order of
magnitude. Year 10: Outcome (§1) met on Afrobarometer measures.

## 11. Kill Condition
If extended-contact cohorts show no attitude shift, or if posting integrity
cannot be enforced, stop and redirect resources to the fair-distribution leg
(Chapters 1/5) — the mechanism would then be judged non-scalable below its
proven tier.

## Irreversibility Test (Rule 7) — the hardest case
**Stock:** cohorts who have lived outside their region; friendships;
inter-ethnic marriages; the posting registry.
**Flow:** everything else. Identity preference decayed across Africa's best
cases — Tanzania −23 points, Ghana −22, Kenya −20 — and Tanzania's ruling
party still campaigns on unity 26 years after Nyerere [Certified]. **No
unity is ever banked.** Budget maintenance permanently, or watch it fade.
