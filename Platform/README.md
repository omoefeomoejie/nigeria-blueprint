**The four commitments** (from `00`): every claim carries its status; the
mistakes file stays public permanently, especially when inconvenient; nobody
is asked to believe anything they cannot check; we say what we do not know.

**The one rule adopted before it costs anything:** no entry in the mistakes
file may ever be removed, softened, or made private. If this platform ever
breaks that rule, the breach alone is sufficient reason to leave it.
