# Chapter 1 — Revenue: Feeding the State From Its People's Work

*Every claim carries its evidence status: [Certified] independent sources;
[Contested] real data, interested interpretation; [Confounded] real outcome,
uncopyable cause; [Owed] the named fact that would settle it.*

---

## 1. Desired Outcome
By year 10 of adoption, a participating state covers the majority of its
operating expenses from its own internally generated revenue (BudgIT Index A
≥ 0.60), sustains capital expenditure at ≥ 60% of budget, and publishes a
citizen-readable ledger converting revenue to delivered projects.
Baseline [Certified]: 30 of 36 states currently depend on federal allocation
for 71–92% of revenue.

## 2. Structural Matches
- **Enugu** [Certified]: IGR ₦37.6bn → ₦180.5bn (2023→24), 5th nationally;
  Index A 0.68; capex reversed to 79–86% of budget; MDA cash collection
  eliminated. A structurally ordinary state escaped dependence.
- **Lagos** [Confounded]: real reform (LIRS, 800k+ properties digitized) —
  inseparable from uncopyable commercial geography. Illustration, not model.
- **Osun** [Contested]: real growth, now independently confirmed in BudgIT's
  top-5 growth index; mechanism story still from interested sources only.
  [Owed: independent mechanism decomposition.]
- **Kwara** [Certified (layer 1 + succession) / Owed (layers 2–3)]: the
  replication case. No endowment; recurring composition (taxes + MDA fees);
  262.8% growth 2020–24 into BudgIT's national top-3 and 5th-least
  FAAC-dependence — on independent numbers. Layer 1 built **by statute**
  (KW-IRS, Revenue Administration Law No. 6 of 2015).

## 3. The Mechanism (what actually transfers)
Not the revenue source — sources are endowments (Lagos's ports, Enugu's
recoverable assets) and do not copy [Certified, Node 1 Rule 4]. The
transferable object is **three institutional layers**:
1. **Collection integrity** — kill cash handling in every MDA; digitize for
   traceability; plug leakage.
2. **Fiscal management** — reverse recurrent-heavy budgets toward capital.
3. **Execution/conversion** — publish revenue-to-delivered-projects so
   collection is never a vanity metric.
Both proof states show identical layers atop opposite sources [Certified].

## 4. Preconditions (Rule 5)
- A governor with the mandate and will to end cash collection over the
  objection of those who live on it.
- A minimum digital-payment infrastructure (banks/POS/USSD reach).
- A believable early return of services, or compliance collapses.
[Partially mapped; full precondition audit is Owed per state.]

## 5. Nigerian Constraints
Taxation split across three tiers; vast informal economy; citizen distrust
("tax is money stolen twice"); the FAAC formula itself rewards dependence
[Certified — the constitutional node inherits this].

## 6. Nigerian Adaptation
No national big-bang. One willing state, 18 months, layers only,
source-agnostic: (a) eliminate MDA cash collection in 6 months; (b) shift
budget ≥ 15 points toward capex in the first budget cycle; (c) publish a
quarterly conversion ledger (₦ collected → projects delivered).

## 7. Political Economy (who loses — and who waits)
Losers: cash-point rentiers, opaque-budget beneficiaries, protected evaders.
They will litigate, frame reform as anti-poor, and — the Kaduna lesson
[Certified, Node 2] — **outwait the founder**. Counter-design: statutory
autonomous revenue authority (not a governor's practice), published
who-pays/who-doesn't, and the poor visibly spared (the target is the
protected evader, not the market trader).

## 8. Early-Win Pilot
Month 6 milestone, publishable: cash collection dead in all MDAs + first
before/after leakage figure + one visible service funded and labeled.
The win is not the money; it is proof the citizen-state bargain works.

## 9. Scale Strategy
Competitive federalism: BudgIT's transparency league already exists
[Certified]; add an autonomy league (Index A + capex share + conversion
ratio). Pressure flows state-to-state; the centre is not required
[units-first — formally Untested, Assumption 1].

## 10. Success Metrics
Year 1: cash-free collection; leakage baseline published. Year 3: IGR +50%
vs baseline; Index A +0.10; capex ≥ 55%. Year 10: Outcome (§1) met.
All figures published, third-party-auditable.

## 11. Kill Condition
If by year 3 revenue rises but the conversion ledger shows no delivery gain,
the citizen-bargain premise has failed — stop, and re-sequence
accountability-first (Node 4 machinery) before resuming.

## Irreversibility Test (Rule 7 — claimed separately)
**Stock** (persists by default): dead cash-handling, digitized rails,
enumerated tax base. **Flow** (dies at succession unless defended): the
autonomous revenue authority and the conversion ledger — must be statutory,
with an organized constituency (the league table + CSO auditors) and an
external anchor. Kaduna's paired case [Certified, Nodes 2/4] is the design
warning; **Kwara is the design proof in this very domain** — its statutory
revenue authority survived the hostile 2019 succession and was reformed,
not scrapped [Certified, v0.12]. The prescription in this section is no
longer theory: it has a named survivor.
