## v0.3 — first evidence-earned commit (origin: Node 1, Enugu certification)

The first engine update paid for by a *new investigation* rather than a review.
Enugu was run as a falsification attempt (search against the thesis, not for it).
The thesis partly failed — which is exactly why this commit matters.

**Earned and added:**
- `Skills/Comparative_Statecraft_Engine.md` → **v0.2**:
  - **Rule 2.1** — decompose revenue by source before classifying (*rule*).
  - **Rule 4** — separate revenue source from the three institutional layers
    (collection integrity / fiscal management / execution) (*rule*).
- `Lessons Learned/Mistakes.md` — entries **1.4** (rejected: "automation drove
  Enugu") and **1.5** (rejected over-correction: "non-tax is suspect").
- `Lessons Learned/Node_1_Decision.md` — first Decision file (*Candidate promoted
  to Earned*). Decision recorded at ~70% confidence.

**Deliberately NOT added (review-disciplined):**
- A rule treating non-tax growth as inherently suspect — *rejected as overreach*;
  it prejudges what decomposition exists to examine. Logged as Mistake 1.5.

**Honest flag:** Node 1 has proven a *mechanism* (the three layers, corroborated)
but not a *transfer* (no ordinary state has yet adopted them from scratch and
succeeded). The Decision records this gap as the still-owed replication proof.
