# Publication Rule — v1.0

Research never ends; publishing has to. This rule protects against Disease B
(never publishing) as Law Zero protects against Disease A (publishing too
early).

**A chapter is publishable when all four hold:**
1. Every major claim carries an Evidence Standard status.
2. No Certified conclusion depends on a Contested claim.
3. Every Contested/Owed claim names the single fact that would close it.
4. The remaining uncertainty would not materially change the chapter's
   recommendations.

Chapters ship with their evidence ledger visible, publish as versioned drafts,
and update by commit when a node moves a status — never silently.
