# Part V — The Movement: Who Carries This

*This is the least evidenced part of the book. Parts II–IV rest on seven
investigations; this part rests on one untested wager and a handful of
findings borrowed from other chapters. We mark it that way rather than
write it with false confidence.*

---

## 1. What this part is not

It is not a call to rally, march, or hate anybody. The Medicine Rule
governs: *blame builds nothing; we did not come to fight anybody, we came
to build.* A movement built on anger recruits fast and dies fast — and in
Nigeria it also gets its members hurt. Everything below is designed to be
carried by people who still have jobs, families, and something to lose.

## 2. The honest status of our central belief

> **The Wager:** when enough of us know the same truth and move, the country
> moves.

**Status: Untested** [Foundations claims ledger]. The 10%-tipping-point
model is folk wisdom, not a finding of this project. We keep it because it
is what we are betting on — and we label it because a book that hides its
weakest claim has no business demanding evidence from anyone else.

What *is* certified: identity and attitude are movable in Nigeria by
**direct contact**, measurably and lastingly [Node 6, NYSC: 15–20pp shifts
persisting ~5 years]. That is the only proven mass-behaviour lever in this
entire book — and it argues for a movement built on *doing things together*,
not on messaging alone.

## 3. The relay, not the audience

Nigeria has both mass illiteracy and a dense, sceptical educated class. So
this Blueprint is not carried by being read by everyone. It moves in a
relay:

**Retellers first** — pastors, imams, teachers, union and market-association
leaders, OAPs, skit-makers, student leaders. They have distribution; they
adopt what makes them sound wise to their own audience. Give them the nine
Doctrine lines as ammunition, not homework.

**Sceptics second** — the educated class does not need to be converted, only
*to fail to break it*. That is what the Research Engine is for: every claim
statused, every reversal recorded, every source named. Their inability to
dismiss it is what keeps the wells clean for the retellers.

**The many third** — reached through the retellers, in Pidgin and mother
tongues, by voice note and radio, recognising rather than being persuaded:
*na so*.

**Implementers last** — a governor, a commissioner, a revenue chairman.
They get a different document: the chapter, the pilot, and the political
cover.

## 4. What a person actually does on Monday

A movement that asks only for belief produces nothing. Contact and action
are what change people [Node 6]. Four tiers, smallest first:

1. **Carry one line.** Use it in a real argument this week. That is the
   whole ask for most people, and it is not trivial: the lines are designed
   so the person who says them wins the exchange.
2. **Check one number.** Every chapter names sources. Look up your own
   state: its IGR, its FAAC dependence, its transparency score, its capital
   expenditure. Publish what you find — a post, a voice note, a WhatsApp
   message. *"Check am"* is not a slogan; it is the assignment.
3. **Join one thing that mixes.** Contact is the proven lever. Serve, host,
   travel, employ, marry, worship, play across the line. This is the only
   instruction in this book with causal evidence behind it.
4. **Adopt one chapter locally.** A market association can demand a
   published revenue-to-delivery ledger. A parents' association can demand
   the assessment scores in Chapter 7. A CSO can take the participatory
   audit in Chapter 4. Chapters are not for governors only; most have a
   citizen-side handle.

## 5. The structure that survives its founders

Everything this project learned about institutions applies to itself
[Rule 7]:

- **Stock:** the published repository — chapters, doctrine, evidence,
  mistakes. Once public and copied, it cannot be quietly withdrawn.
- **Flow:** the investigations, the version releases, the corrections. These
  die by default unless housed with more than one person and defended by a
  constituency.

So: the work is public and copyable by design; contributors are recruited to
run nodes; and no claim depends on any individual's reputation, because
every claim is checkable without trusting anyone. **A movement whose truth
depends on its founder is a personality cult with citations.**

## 6. Political economy of the movement itself

The organized losers of Chapters 1–7 will not attack the evidence — that is
hard. They will attack the carrier: the motive, the funding, the ethnicity,
the ambition [Rule 6, generalized]. Counter-design, stated in advance:
publish the methodology before the conclusions; refuse the ethnic frame in
every rendering (Doctrine line 8); name no enemy; and keep the Mistakes file
public, so the first person to prove us wrong finds we already record our
own reversals.

## 7. Kill conditions for this part

- If after two years the Doctrine lines are not being repeated by people who
  never met us, the relay model has failed — redesign distribution.
- If the movement's growth comes with an ethnic or religious skew, we have
  become a faction, not a movement — stop and rebuild the neutrality.
- If any chapter's evidence is refuted and the movement defends it anyway,
  the project has become an ideology and has failed on its own Preamble.

## 8. What we owe

Part V will remain the thinnest part of this book until it earns evidence:
adoption data, retelling data, a pilot actually run. **Owed:** any measured
instance of the Wager working — and the honest possibility that it does not.
