# The Nine Lines — Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo

## ⚠ Status: DRAFT. Do not circulate.

These renderings were produced without a native speaker. They are here so
that a Hausa, Yoruba or Igbo speaker has something to correct rather than a
blank page. **Every line below is assumed wrong until a native speaker signs
it off.**

Why the caution: these lines are meant to carry like proverbs and survive ten
retellings. That depends entirely on rhythm and idiom, which is exactly what
a non-speaker cannot judge. A line that is technically accurate and rhythmically
dead is worse than no line, because it will not travel and it will make the
speaker sound foreign to their own audience.

**What a reviewer should do:** ignore literal fidelity. Ask only — *would a
person say this, and would another person repeat it?* If the answer needs an
existing proverb substituted for our sentence, substitute it. Meaning is the
constraint; wording is not.

## 1. The Feeding Rule
**EN:** A government that eats from your work must answer your voice. A
government that eats oil answers nobody.
**PG:** Government wey chop from your work must answer you. Government wey
chop oil no send anybody.
**HA (draft):** Gwamnatin da ke ci daga aikinka dole ta amsa maka. Gwamnatin
da ke cin man fetur ba ta amsa kowa.
**YO (draft):** Ìjọba tí ó ń jẹ nínú iṣẹ́ rẹ gbọ́dọ̀ dá ọ lóhùn. Ìjọba tí ó ń
jẹ epo kò dá ẹnikẹ́ni lóhùn.
**IG (draft):** Ọchịchị na-eri site n'ọrụ gị ga-aza gị. Ọchịchị na-eri mmanụ
adịghị aza onye ọ bụla.

## 2. The Three Jobs
**EN / PG:** Block the leak. Plan the money. Show the work.
**HA (draft):** Toshe ɗigar kuɗi. Tsara kuɗin. Nuna aikin.
**YO (draft):** Dí ojú ẹ̀gbin. Ṣètò owó náà. Fi iṣẹ́ náà hàn.
**IG (draft):** Mechie ebe ego na-agbapụta. Hazie ego ahụ. Gosi ọrụ ahụ.

## 3. The Builder's Rule
**EN:** Don't build on one good man. Build what a bad man cannot scatter.
**PG:** No build on top one good man. Build wetin bad man no fit scatter.
**HA (draft):** Kada ka gina a kan mutum ɗaya nagari. Gina abin da mugu ba
zai iya rushewa ba.
**YO (draft):** Má kọ́ lé ẹnì kan rere. Kọ́ ohun tí ènìyàn burúkú kò lè tú
ká.
**IG (draft):** Ewula ewu n'elu otu ezigbo mmadụ. Wuo ihe ajọ mmadụ na-agaghị
akwatu.

## 4. The Seat Rule
**EN:** Test the person before the seat, and let the law hold the test, not
the oga.
**PG:** Test person before seat — na law go hold the test, no be oga.
**HA (draft):** A gwada mutum kafin kujera, kuma doka ce za ta riƙe jarrabawar,
ba shugaba ba.
**YO (draft):** Dán ènìyàn wò kí ó tó jókòó. Òfin ni kí ó di ìdánwò náà mú,
kì í ṣe ọ̀gá.
**IG (draft):** Nwalee mmadụ tupu oche. Ka iwu jide ule ahụ, ọ bụghị oga.

## 5. The Trace Rule
**EN:** Money you can trace is money that can reach you.
**PG:** Money wey you fit trace na money wey fit reach you.
**HA (draft):** Kuɗin da za ka iya bibiya, shi ne kuɗin da zai iya isa gare
ka.
**YO (draft):** Owó tí o lè tọpasẹ̀ ni owó tí ó lè dé ọ̀dọ̀ rẹ.
**IG (draft):** Ego ị nwere ike ịchọpụta bụ ego nwere ike iru gị aka.

## 6. The Ground and the Badge
**EN:** Safety comes when those who know the ground join those who hold the
power, under law.
**PG:** Person wey sabi ground and person wey carry badge go join hand —
under law.
**HA (draft):** Tsaro na zuwa ne idan masu sanin ƙasa suka haɗa hannu da masu
riƙe da iko, ƙarƙashin doka.
**YO (draft):** Ààbò dé nígbà tí àwọn tí ó mọ ilẹ̀ bá para pọ̀ mọ́ àwọn tí ó
di agbára mú, lábẹ́ òfin.
**IG (draft):** Nchekwa na-abịa mgbe ndị maara ala jikọrọ aka na ndị ji ike,
n'okpuru iwu.

## 7. The Doors Rule
**EN:** Every wall has a door somewhere. Take the open doors first.
**PG:** Every wall get door. Enter the open ones first.
**HA (draft):** Kowane bango yana da ƙofa. Ka fara shiga ta buɗaɗɗun ƙofofi.
**YO (draft):** Gbogbo ògiri ní ilẹ̀kùn níbìkan. Wọlé ní àwọn tí ó ṣí sílẹ̀
ná.
**IG (draft):** Mgbidi ọ bụla nwere ọnụ ụzọ. Buru ụzọ banye ndị meghere emeghe.

## 8. The Medicine Rule
**EN:** Blame builds nothing. We did not come to fight anybody, we came to
build.
**PG:** Blame no dey build road. We no come fight person; we come build.
**HA (draft):** Zargi ba ya gina komai. Ba mu zo yaƙi da kowa ba; mun zo mu
gina.
**YO (draft):** Ẹ̀bi kì í kọ́ nǹkan kan. A kò wá bá ẹnikẹ́ni jà; a wá kọ́ ilé.
**IG (draft):** Ịta ụta anaghị ewu ihe ọ bụla. Anyị abịaghị ịlụso onye ọ bụla
ọgụ; anyị bịara iwu ihe.

## 9. The Signature
**EN:** Don't believe us, check it. Everything is written, so anybody can
test it.
**PG:** No believe us — check am. Everything dey written; anybody fit test am.
**HA (draft):** Kada ka gaskata mu — ka duba. An rubuta kome, kowa na iya
gwadawa.
**YO (draft):** Má gbà wá gbọ́ — yẹ̀ ẹ́ wò. A ti kọ gbogbo rẹ̀ sílẹ̀; ẹnikẹ́ni
lè dán an wò.
**IG (draft):** Ekwela anyị — lelee ya. E deela ihe niile; onye ọ bụla nwere
ike ịnwale ya.

## The Wager *(hope, not proof)*
**EN:** When enough of us know the same truth and move, the country moves.
**PG:** When enough of us sabi the same thing and move, naija go move.
**HA (draft):** Idan isassun mu suka san gaskiya ɗaya suka motsa, ƙasar za ta
motsa.
**YO (draft):** Nígbà tí ó bá tó nínú wa mọ òtítọ́ kan náà tí a sì gbéra,
orílẹ̀-èdè yóò gbéra.
**IG (draft):** Mgbe ọtụtụ n'ime anyị matara otu eziokwu ma kwaga, obodo ga-akwaga.
