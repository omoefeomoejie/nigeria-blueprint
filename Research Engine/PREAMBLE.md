# Preamble — The Highest Principle

> The purpose of this project is not to prove what we believe.
> It is to discover what is true, even when the truth forces us to
> abandon our own assumptions.
>
> Every conclusion in this Blueprint is provisional. Each one must
> remain open to revision the moment better evidence appears.

This sentence sits above every law, skill, and chapter. It exists because the
most common death of a national-reform movement is not lack of intelligence —
it is emotional attachment to its own theory. A movement that cannot change its
mind in public becomes an ideology, and ideology cannot govern reality.

The Research Engine institutionalises one rare habit: changing our mind in
response to evidence, on the record, with the reversal preserved rather than
quietly deleted.
