# Level 2 — How We Know

*Structured written answers. Ten questions, answer any six.*
**Pass mark: 50%.** Marked on reasoning, not agreement.

---

**1.** A governor announces that his reform doubled his state's revenue.
Place each of the following at the right confidence level and justify it:
(a) the revenue figure itself, (b) the explanation of why it rose, (c) the
claim that his reform caused it. *(Ch 25)*

**2.** Why is the most famous case in any field usually the one you should
trust least? Use Lagos and Vietnam. *(Ch 26)*

**3.** "IGR went up 4.8 times." Explain why this is not yet a finding, using
what happened when we broke Enugu's number apart. *(Ch 26)*

**4.** We withdrew Osun's growth claim ourselves. Explain what we did to the
number and why the same problem affects almost every Nigerian figure quoted
between 2023 and 2025. *(Ch 26)*

**5.** A state agency publishes both a figure and an explanation of that
figure. Explain why these deserve different treatment, using Osun. *(Ch 26)*

**6.** Kyrgyzstan copied Georgia's police reform exactly and it failed. What
was missing, and what question does that force you to ask about any policy
you want to copy? *(Ch 26)*

**7.** A survey shows people feel safer. Recorded deaths rose over the same
period. Explain why neither settles the question, and what evidence would.
*(Ch 26)*

**8.** Apply the three questions — who is counting, would it survive the next
government, what would prove it wrong — to a real programme announced in your
own state. *(Ch 27)*

**9.** Describe how you would audit your own state in one afternoon. Name at
least four things you would look for and say what each would tell you.
*(Ch 28)*

**10.** This movement examines its members, and so does the Chinese Communist
Party. Explain, using our own findings, why we keep the exam and reject the
model. *(Ch 30)*

---

## What markers are told

Award marks for **reasoning that reaches a sound conclusion**, in whatever
words the candidate uses, in English or Pidgin.

Do **not** award marks for reproducing the book's phrasing without the
reasoning behind it.

Do **not** deduct marks for a candidate who reaches a different conclusion
than the book, **provided the reasoning is sound and the evidence is handled
honestly.** A candidate who correctly identifies a weakness in our position
is demonstrating exactly what this level exists to test.
