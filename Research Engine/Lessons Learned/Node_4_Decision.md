# Node 4 — Decision

**Node:** Accountability
**Question investigated:** Has any Nigerian state built a transparency/
accountability mechanism that measurably reduced leakage or improved delivery —
and did it survive succession?
**Status:** Decision-grade.

---

## The decision

**Accountability machinery is buildable, independently certifiable, and
succession-survivable in Nigeria — when statutorily housed with an organized
constituency. The transparency→outcome link is internationally certified but
unmeasured in Nigeria; the Blueprint chapter must mandate outcome measurement
from day one rather than assume it.**

**Confidence: ~70%.** High on machinery + succession (independent BudgIT 39/39
under the successor; Rule 7 paired case). Priced for the empty Nigerian outcome
leg.

---

## Evidence ledger

**Certified (independent):**
- Kaduna: first subnational OGP member (2018); BudgIT SFTL Q4-2025 maximum
  39/39 (one of nine states); IRM-era ranking first of 36 on budget+contract
  access; CoST membership; FOI domesticated; portal disclosing 1,379 projects
  (₦95.7bn by Apr 2025).
- **Succession survival (Rule 7 paired case):** the same successor who let the
  competency-test flow die kept and extended the OGP flow (2024–25 action plan
  co-created with 100+ CSOs; OCDS→OC4IDS upgrade; 5% capital ring-fence;
  Citizen Participatory Audit framework 2025). Difference: statutory housing
  (KADPPA/procurement law) + organized external constituency + international
  lock-in.
- Comparative: Lagos's procurement portal non-functional despite budget
  disclosures — endowment does not buy the accountability machine (Rule 4
  echo in a fourth domain).
- Mechanism (international research): bid transparency significantly increases
  competition; pre-award transparency cuts corruption risk; EU savings est.
  2.3–3.5%; firms (esp. small) participate more in transparent systems.
- Rationale: >60% of Nigerian corruption cases are procurement-linked.

**Contested:**
- Rigasa contract-revocation loop (OGP-sourced anecdote).
- Usability (no citizens' budget — state's own CS co-chair) and whether
  participation binds decisions (BudgIT warning, 2026).

**Owed:**
- Any measured Nigerian leakage-reduction/savings/competition figure
  (confirmed absent after three drills — Nigeria measures whether the machine
  is on, not what it saves).

---

## Recommendation
1. Accountability chapter = machinery (proven) + **mandated outcome
   measurement designed in from day one** (Ukraine-style savings tracking),
   per Rule 8: an unmeasured mechanism cannot anchor a checkable Blueprint.
2. Succession design per Rule 7: statutory housing + organized constituency +
   external anchor (OGP/CoST-class lock-in) are the survival kit; replicate
   deliberately.
3. Pair with Node 1's execution layer: transparency of the *conversion* of
   revenue to delivered projects is where Nodes 1 and 4 fuse.
4. Proceed per the five-step sequence: Chapter 1 (Revenue), then Node 5
   (Constitutional).
