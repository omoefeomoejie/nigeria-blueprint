# The Doctrine — v0.1

*The short layer. What a person can carry in the mouth and win an argument
with at a joint. Every line has a written chapter behind it that anybody can
open and check. That is the whole difference between this and every promise
Nigeria has heard.*

**Rules this text obeys.** Lines carry like proverbs. They survive ten
retellings without changing shape. They speak to you, not about Abuja. No
line names an enemy. Every line traces to a finding you can check, and every
claim invites you to go and check it.

---

## The Nine Lines

**1. The Feeding Rule**
EN: *A government that eats from your work must answer your voice. A
government that eats oil answers nobody.*
PG: *Government wey chop from your work must answer you. Government wey chop
oil no send anybody.*

**2. The Three Jobs** *(what every good government does with money)*
EN & PG: *Block the leak. Plan the money. Show the work.*

**3. The Builder's Rule**
EN: *Don't build on one good man. Build what a bad man cannot scatter.*
PG: *No build on top one good man. Build wetin bad man no fit scatter.*

**4. The Seat Rule**
EN: *Test the person before the seat — and let the law hold the test, not
the oga.*
PG: *Test person before seat — and na law go hold the test, no be oga.*

**5. The Trace Rule**
EN: *Money you can trace is money that can reach you.*
PG: *Money wey you fit trace na money wey fit reach you.*

**6. The Ground and the Badge**
EN: *Safety comes when those who know the ground join those who hold the
power — under law.*
PG: *Person wey sabi ground and person wey carry badge go join hand — under
law.*

**7. The Doors Rule**
EN: *Every wall has a door somewhere. Take the open doors first.*
PG: *Every wall get door. Enter the open ones first.*

**8. The Medicine Rule**
EN: *Blame builds nothing. We did not come to fight anybody; we came to
build.*
PG: *Blame no dey build road. We no come fight person; we come build.*

**9. The Signature**
EN: *Don't believe us — check it. Everything is written, so anybody can test
it.*
PG: *No believe us — check am. Everything dey written; anybody fit test am.*

## Candidate line 10 — The Scorekeeper *(new, awaiting the retelling test)*

**EN:** *If nobody outside is counting, nothing is being fixed.*
**PG:** *If person wey no dey inside no dey count am, dem never fix am.*

Backed by: the sixteen-domain scorekeeper test. Five failures, every one a
place where government does the thing and reports on the thing; eleven
passes, every one with somebody outside counting [Certified].

Marked as a candidate rather than adopted, because a doctrine line must
survive ten retellings and this one has not been tested on a single human
being. If it does not carry, the finding stays in the Blueprint and out of
the doctrine.

## The Wager *(this one is hope, not proof, and we say so)*

EN: *When enough of us know the same truth and move, the country moves.*
PG: *When enough of us sabi the same thing and move, naija go move.*

Status: **Untested**. The tipping-point idea is folk wisdom, not a finding of
this project. It stays here because it is what we are betting on, and it
stays labelled because a book that hides its weakest claim has no business
demanding evidence from anybody else. If the movement proves it or breaks
it, this section changes.

## Rendering Rules
1. One doctrine, several renderings — never several doctrines. Hausa, Yoruba,
   Igbo renderings translate these lines with native rhythm, same meaning.
2. Native distribution unit: the **60-second voice note** and the
   radio-readable card. Audio is the printing press of an oral country.
3. Retellers first: lines are ammunition for pulpits, minbars, radio,
   classrooms, parks. A line that cannot survive ten retellings is redrafted.
4. No line is ever weaponized against a named person. The Medicine Rule
   governs all use.

## Revision Rule
Doctrine lines change only when the nodes behind them change, by commit,
never silently — same as everything else in this repository.
